# V0.guard-mechanism: the resource guard's types live in one package that the mechanism and the policy both import, and cgroup implements the guard's Mechanism; nothing the guard does changes

→ decision 0029, decision 0032, decision 0036, V0.86, V0.163, V0.164, V0.services-not-jobs. First slice of #458.

The guard had no single owner. Its types were in `internal/slots` (signals, needs, holders, the guard's view), and `internal/cgroup`, the mechanism that freezes and kills, imported `slots` and `internal/heavy` (policy) to use them. That is the wrong direction: the policy cannot move without the mechanism moving with it. This change turns it around and changes no decision. The 17 scenarios of the load simulation (V0.load-sim) give the same table before and after, and their invariants hold the same.

(1) **`internal/guard/resource`: the vocabulary.** `Signals` (and how they read: `Critical`, `Fresh`, `NearOomd`, `Reserve`), `Stall`, `Policy`, `Need`, `Factors`, `Extra`, `Holder`, `Undeclared`, `Snapshot`, the guard's published view (`Guard`, `GuardEvent`, `GuardThread`, `GuardRerun`, `WorkUnit`, `Invariants`), the kinds (`test`, `build`, `e2e`), and the view types of the heavy and service patterns (`Added`, `ServicePattern`) move here from `slots` and `heavy`. How a class's need is counted (`NeedOf`, `Prior`, `LearnRuns`) moves too, because admission and the job limit must count it alike (decision 0029 section 2). The package imports nothing of Fyr's. Every caller now names `resource.X` where it named `slots.X`; no alias is left behind.

(2) **cgroup no longer imports the policy.** `Sampler` reads the pool through a `cgroup.Pool` interface (`Off`, `N`, `Snapshot`, `View`) and the books through `AddedBook` and `ServiceBook`; `*slots.Pool`, `*heavy.Book` and `*heavy.Services` satisfy them, so the wiring is unchanged. `ComposeProject`, which only `cgroup` calls, moves from `heavy` into `cgroup`. A holder's grant channel stays inside the pool (an unexported `waiter`), so `resource.Holder` is plain data. `Snapshot.Bound`, read only by the AIMD step, is a field instead of a method. `internal/guard`'s `TestLayering` fails if `cgroup` imports `slots`, `heavy`, `admission`, `engine` or `guard`, or if `guard` imports a mechanism.

(3) **`guard.Mechanism`.** `internal/guard` declares what the policy asks of a host: `Sample`, `Freeze` and `Thaw` (a job), `FreezeUndeclared` and `ThawUndeclared`, and `Kill`. `cgroup.Mechanism` implements it over the job groups, the sampler and the detector; on a host without the cgroup tree the job groups stop process trees (`TreeReader`, SIGSTOP), so the same type serves macOS and Linux without cgroups. The two wiring closures that froze and thawed undeclared work are its methods now, and the engine's `SlotSignals`, `SlotFreeze`, `SlotThaw`, `UndeclaredFreeze`, `UndeclaredThaw` and `JobKill` are wired from it.

(4) **What is left for the next slices of #458.** These are not done here: (the first is done in V0.guard-tick, the rest in V0.guard-finish, which says what is still left)
- The policy moves into `internal/guard` as one pure `Tick(signals, holders, jobs) → []Decision{admit, freeze, thaw, kill, notify}`: `slots`' admission rules (`DecideWith`, `EASY`, `Thrashing`, the AIMD), `internal/admission`, and the engine's `freezeGuard` with `guarddecide.go` and `tunables.go`. The engine then only turns notify decisions into Inputs.
- The job-limit sizing in `cgroup/joblimit.go` (which reads `Prior` and `LearnRuns`) becomes a decision of that policy instead of a choice the mechanism makes.
- A process-table Mechanism of its own, split out of `JobGroups`' `canStop` branches, so cgroup's type is cgroups only.
- The rest of `cmd/fyrd/wire_guard.go`'s closures (the roots' memory, the work units, the rotation, the rerun wall) move behind the Mechanism or into the policy, so the file holds wiring only.

(5) **A finding for the policy slice, not acted on here.** Since release `3be8b2f` the freezes at high headroom are stalls of the kernel reclaiming file cache in the agents' slice, not a shortage of anonymous memory (V0.agents-budget-share (6)). A cutoff on the working set cannot tell those apart from the desktop freeze of 20 Nov at 64 % headroom (V0.freeze-fast-class-name), so the rule V0.agents-budget-share tried was dropped. The new policy should take a signal that separates the two (for example the agents' file-cache refaults against their anonymous growth) instead of the working set alone. This change keeps the guard's behaviour as it is.
