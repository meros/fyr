# V0.sim-fixes: the guard keeps a frozen job's hold, never freezes the last heavy work or on the headroom alone, a job's limit grows with its hits, and a cheap job backfills while memory is short

→ amends V0.guard-wake (3), V0.multi-freeze, V0.job-limits (4), V0.164 section 7, decision 0032 slice 1, decision 0029 S2 and decision 0036 (C), (E) 4. Fixes what V0.load-sim (4) found.

(1) **A frozen job keeps its hold.** `SlotActive` does not move while a job is frozen, so `sweepSlots` read its hold as idle and dropped it: a foreground call at `HeavyCallStale` (15 min), a background job at `SlotIdle` (2 min), and after a restart any frozen job at 2 min, since `heavyCalls` is empty then. `freezeGuard` then thawed it as "episode or hold ended" into the critical stall it was frozen for (file-cache, two-growers, background, restart-frozen). Now `sweepSlots` passes over a hold the guard holds frozen, and the hold's idle time starts at its thaw. A hold or episode that ends while its work is frozen and still there (a call's result came) ends, but the work thaws only when the stall is below critical, or at `FreezeCeiling` (log: `keeping thread=… frozen: its hold or episode ended …`). At start V0.thaw-at-start still thaws everything and the new engine holds nothing frozen, so nothing here changes there.

(2) **A job's limit grows with it.** A class learned at 1.5 GB whose jobs grow to 7 GB got ×1.2 on its class once and kept a third of its memory, about 30× slower, holding the agents' stall critical; neither job finished in 2 h. Now each hit (`memory.events` high rises) raises the job's own `memory.high` to its use × 1.25, at every hit, with no limit at or past the agents' budget (log: `why=raised`); the class is still raised once per job. While the job's own `memory.pressure` full avg10 is 10 % or more (`StallCritical`) its limit lifts (`why=own-stall`): it is throttling itself into the stall V0.job-limits meant to avoid; its use × 1.25 is the least the limit comes back at. While the headroom is short, admission now lets a cheap job (both shares at most `CheapShare`) start unless the stall is critical, near oomd's limit or the agents' swap is full (decision 0029 section 4: it barely touches the head's room). It did not count the headroom without a job that sits at its own limit: with the limit raised at each hit and lifted under its own stall a job no longer sits there, and that memory is in use.

(3) **One heavy work always runs.** `maxFrozen` was the running threads less one, so agents with no heavy work let every heavy part be frozen; the frozen memory stayed charged and only the 10 min thaw ran the work again, into the same shortage (leak, swap-full, near-oomd, turns-4x4GB). Now:
- at most the threads with heavy work (a hold or a flag) less one are frozen (log: `at most 1 (threads with heavy work less one)`);
- undeclared work is not frozen whole when it is the only heavy work that runs; work of two or more parts still takes turns, since one part runs. An idle agent beside it is no longer a reason (decision 0032 slice 1 froze it then). Undeclared work is still frozen before a job (V0.164 section 7), and the oldest hold still never;
- when the frozen work is all the heavy work left, the oldest freeze wakes at once unless the stall is critical (`reason="no other heavy work runs"`);
- the timed thaw at `FreezeMax` waits while what of the work is out of RAM (its memory at the freeze less the thread's memory now) would not fit above the reserve; it comes back with the thaw;
- the headroom alone freezes nothing: a freeze needs the host's or the agents' stall at elevated or worse, a critical stall, or the unit near oomd's limit (log: `not freezing on the headroom alone: memory does not stall …`). A short headroom with no stall means the kernel copes (page cache, swap), and the frozen memory stays charged; the gate still holds new heavy commands. A freeze needs short headroom and at least an elevated stall, the agents' or the host's; admission still holds new jobs on the headroom alone. In the simulation this took near-oomd from 3 freezes and 13 min to 1 and 9 min, and swap-full from 12 freezes to 2.

(4) **The wake rule of turns counts the frozen parts.** Work taking turns was woken by the thread's memory at the freeze, which counted the part whose turn it is (already in the agents' memory) and the parts that ended since: with one part left it read "agents=0.8 GB + its 11.2 GB >= cap=12.0 GB" and waited for the 10 min limit. The freeze now keeps each frozen part's memory at its freeze; the rule adds those of the parts still there and takes their memory now off the agents'.

(5) **Not changed: oomd and frozen pages.** In swap-full oomd still kills a frozen thread: the two parts that run fill the 2 GB of swap and the stall reaches oomd's limit, with or without Fyr (no Fyr loses a thread too), and the model counts the kernel's reclaim of the frozen part's cold pages as its pgscan. Fyr's own reclaim is already off within 10 points of the limit (V0.guard-tidy); the kernel's is not Fyr's to stop, short of not freezing, and how real oomd ranks a frozen cgroup is not measured. It stays in the simulation's `known` list.

(6) **The simulation.** Its host now runs V0.freeze-charge by default (`ReclaimReachesCLI`, `LimitFloor`; the rows turns-old-reclaim and unknown-limit-old run the tree before it) and models (2). The max-frozen invariant counts the threads with heavy work, a thread taking turns as running. Every scenario but swap-full's oomd kill now fails the test on a violation. Before (with V0.wake-once and V0.freeze-charge) and after, minutes and seconds of critical stall: two-growers did not finish → 51 min (2811 s); background did not finish → 65 min; restart-frozen did not finish → 46 min; file-cache 74 → 38 min; leak 92 → 63 min; huge-job 242 → 44 min; near-oomd 13 → 9 min.

(7) **With V0.freeze-fast-class-name and V0.load-invariants.** A fresh PSI trigger is a critical stall, so it is a stall for "the headroom alone freezes nothing", and it keeps frozen work left alone frozen as any critical stall does, for at most `TriggerFresh` (a wake into it would be V0.load-invariants' thaw-into-critical). The work the trigger flags early is heavy work like any flag: `maxFrozen` counts it, and it is not frozen whole while it is the only heavy work that runs. V0.load-invariants' `short-nothing-frozen` counts only while the guard may freeze: memory stalls (elevated or worse, a fresh trigger, near oomd's limit) and the flagged work runs beside other heavy work or in two or more parts; short on the headroom alone, or with the only heavy work that runs, nothing frozen keeps the rules above. In the simulation the max-frozen invariant allows the extra frozen thread only while the guard's signals read critical or near oomd's limit, the rule above. The host's PSI trigger is now 200 ms in 2 s, fyrd's (it modelled 150 ms). With both: near-oomd 3 freezes and 10 min (the trigger freezes twice more after the first thaw; 49 s of critical stall, 70 s without Fyr), unknown-limit 2 freezes and 83 s of critical stall (19 s with the 150 ms trigger), unknown-limit-old 85 s; the other rows are unchanged.
