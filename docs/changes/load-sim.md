# V0.load-sim: a simulation runs Fyr's real admission and memory guard against a modelled host over 17 scenarios and checks invariants

→ decisions 0029, 0032 and 0036, V0.multi-freeze, V0.guard-wake, V0.oomd-guard, V0.guard-tidy, V0.job-limits. (owner, 19 Nov: "Can we simulate the logic and see if this will be well behaved in various situations?")

(1) **What runs.** `internal/engine/sim_test.go` builds an engine as fyrd wires it: `slots.Pool` with `internal/admission`'s policy, the AIMD budget, `HeavyGate`, and the engine tick with `freezeGuard`, the turns, the wake rule and `memoryGuard`. The PSI trigger calls `GuardNow`. Every dependency (signals, freeze and thaw, the parts of undeclared work, the job tracker, the detector) is answered by the host model in `simhost_test.go`. A step is one simulated second and the engine ticks every 10. It is a test, so it ships nothing: `go test ./internal/engine/ -run TestSim -v` prints one row per scenario in about 5 s. `FYR_SIM_LOG=<scenario>` prints that run's `guard:` log.

(2) **The host model.** The model is 8 cores, 12 GB of `memory.high` for the agents, 24 GB of RAM with the desktop as a scenario input, and 8 GB of swap cap read back at 400 MB/s. Running parts that do not fit keep the same share phi of their memory and run 1 + 51.6 (1−phi)^1.44 times slower: 8× at 75 % and 20× at 50 %, as decision 0032 measured. Anon that swap cannot take is a livelock. A part counts as fully stalled 1 − m^−0.5 of its time, so the 18 Nov incident reads about 40 % against the measured 53 %. oomd kills the thread with the most pages scanned after 20 s over its limit. A frozen part keeps its pages until the kernel moves cold pages (200 MB/s) or Fyr's reclaim does. That reclaim does not reach pages charged to `cli/`. Job limits are V0.job-limits'. Two switches model fixes that are not in this tree: `ReclaimReachesCLI` and `LimitFloor` (an unknown class's limit is at least the prior). These are fits, not measurements.

(3) **The invariants.** No oomd kill while another thread's heavy work ran unfrozen, or of work the guard froze. No thaw into the agents' critical stall before 30 min of work that had not ended. At most the running threads less one frozen. Never the oldest running hold, never a service. Some heavy work runs within every 60 s. Every job finishes. No part is frozen more than 10 times outside turns. Each row also gives the time to finish all jobs with no Fyr (all at once) and serially, the most freezes of one part, the longest wait in the queue, the seconds of critical stall with and without Fyr, and the peak swap.

(4) **What it found on today's code.** These violations are listed in `known` with their reasons. They are logged and do not fail the test, so the simulation could merge before the fixes. Remove a line when its fix lands:
1. *A frozen job loses its hold.* `SlotActive` does not move while the job is frozen. `sweepSlots` drops the hold of a foreground call at `HeavyCallStale` (15 min) and of a background job at `SlotIdle` (2 min). After a restart it drops any frozen job's hold after 2 min, because `heavyCalls` is empty. `freezeGuard` then thaws the job as "episode or hold ended" into a critical stall (file-cache, two-growers, background, restart-frozen).
2. *A job limit from a wrong learned peak starves the job.* A class learned at 1.5 GB that grows to 7 GB keeps a third of its memory. A hit raises the peak by 1.2 once. The job runs about 30× slower and holds the agents' stall critical (two-growers, background). An unknown class is capped at half the budget even when it runs alone (huge-job: 242 min against 44 without Fyr). Meanwhile a 0.5 GB job waits 239 min, because a short memory holds every job.
3. *Everything can be frozen.* `maxFrozen` counts agents that have no heavy work, and undeclared work is frozen for their sake. The frozen memory stays charged, so the headroom never recovers and only the 10 min timed thaw runs the work again. That thaw sees a calm stall because nothing runs (leak, near-oomd, swap-full, turns-4x4GB). In near-oomd and unknown-limit the guard froze on headroom alone while nothing stalled.
4. *The wake rule cannot fire during turns.* A frozen thread's memory includes the part whose turn it is, so it is counted twice ("agents=12.0 GB + its 11.2 GB >= cap=12.0 GB").
5. *The limit at the move.* Work moved into `undeclared/` while memory is short gets the free budget as `memory.high`. After the thaw it crawls under that limit, and the stall this causes sets a wake cap it never fits under (unknown-limit 118 min; with the floor 20 min).
6. *oomd kills frozen work.* The kernel's reclaim of a frozen thread's cold pages counts as pgscan, which makes that thread oomd's first pick (swap-full, near-oomd).
7. *Reclaim and turns.* With reclaim reaching `cli/`, the turns case ends in 11 min instead of 36. The model's gain is larger than decision 0032 measured, so read it as the direction, not the size.
