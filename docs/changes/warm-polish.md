# V0.warm-polish: a warm, calm finish on both fronts, the agent's replies in bubbles, and no coloured side stripes

→ base.css tokens, V0.section-bands, V0.empty-section-bands, V0.row-calm (5), V0.149 (the Run card). *Amends V0.section-bands (1) and V0.empty-section-bands (1): a section header has no band. Amends V0.row-calm (5): the age and the CPU reading are tabular figures in the UI face, not monospace.*

Why: the client was usable but read as cool and busy: slate neutrals, monospace on every time and count, hard 1 px frames on every card, five kinds of filled chips in a thread's header, coloured left stripes to mark a card's state, and the agent's words loose on the page while the owner's sat in bubbles. Three rounds of design and an adversarial visual review on the mock screens (both fronts, light and dark) led to this finish.

1. **Warm tokens.** The neutrals move from slate to paper and sand in light (`--surface` #f7f4ef, `--raised` #fffdfa) and to warm charcoal in dark (`--surface` #191714, `--raised` #1f1c19). The primary fill (`--night`) is espresso in light and cream in dark. The status hues keep their meaning (amber needs you, red failed, green fine, blue link and focus). `--ink-faint` meets WCAG AA on the page in both themes. Shadows (`--lift-*`) are warm and soft. The PWA's theme colour follows `--surface`. The project palette is unchanged.
2. **Numbers in the UI face.** Ages, counts, token and CPU readings, durations, model names and quota figures use Geist with tabular figures. Monospace stays for paths, branches, commands, code and output.
3. **Shapes and lines.** Rows, cards and option cards have 12 px corners, the question card 16 px, dialogs 18 px; chips are pills. Cards lift with a shadow instead of a strong frame. A question's options size to their words, so an option without a description is not half empty.
4. **Section headers without a band.** The title, its icon and its count set a section apart, with the air above it as before; the band behind it is gone on the desktop and the phone alike. Hovering a foldable header still shades it.
5. **No coloured side stripes.** A card's state is never a coloured left edge: the home screen's Your-move cards carry it in the amber action word ("2 questions", "Merge #4170"), a Run card in its result line, a message from another thread and a failed setting in their own words and wash.
6. **The agent's replies are bubbles.** Each reply of the agent is one bubble on the left (a command it wrote stays inside it), as the owner's messages are bubbles on the right. Its process notes, tool lines, run summaries and Fyr's lines stay plain, as V0.21 keeps machine lines quiet.
7. **Quiet chrome.** In a thread's header only the state chip and the PR chip keep a fill; the others are outlines, on the phone as on the desktop. The home screen has one primary action (the first card's); the others are outlined. A disabled button reads as waiting, not as a grey slab. Fields and the dialog's × focus with a soft ring.
8. **Where it lives.** The tokens are in `web/src/base.css` and the lifts in `layout.css`; the component finish is one layer, `web/src/warm.css`, last in `app.css`. The design system's generated tokens (`design/system/`) are not regenerated here.
