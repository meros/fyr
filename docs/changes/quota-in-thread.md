# V0.quota-in-thread: the provider quota shows in the open thread's header, and a turn names no cost

→ V0.44, V0.152 (5), V0.167 (2), V0.166 (2), V0.21, V0.206. *Amends V0.44 (3) (the rail's footer), V0.152 (5) and V0.167 (2) (the rail's quota grid goes) and V0.21 (the end of a turn).* (owner, 18 Nov: "We need to show the provider quota in a better place, where it's more visible during daily work. But also not in the way... Also, remove the cost per tokens stuff since these are subs they don't carry API costs." The owner chose the thread's header, the rail's grid removed, and the turn cost removed everywhere.) What was wrong: on the desktop the quota was an initial and one figure at the bottom of the rail, 10 px high; on the phone it showed only in New thread and in the host-load sheet. The end of a turn showed the CLI's `total_cost_usd`, which is what the API would charge, not what a subscription pays.

(1) **Desktop.** The header of the open thread has a quota chip after the model and the effort: every window of the provider this thread runs on, on its host ("5 h 34 % · 7 d 61 %"), or the balance of a key provider ("¥12.40 left"). A window at 80 % or more is red (V0.44's rule); stale data is dimmed. Hover shows each window's reset time in local time, when the data was read, and the stale or error note. A provider with an error and no data says "No quota reading", with the error on hover. A provider without quota (kind `none`), or a token account before its first run (V0.122), shows no chip.

(2) **Phone.** The title bar of the open thread shows one figure before the ⋯ button, the busiest window's share ("61 %", red from 80 %), or the balance. It is 44 px high, and a tap opens the thread's Details, which has a Quota row with what the desktop's hover says: each window with its reset time, the age and the notes. The reason for the difference: the phone's chip row is one line of at most 360 px that the project, the state, the host, the branch, the provider and the PR already fill (a figure there was pushed out by a PR chip), and a phone has no hover.

(3) **The rail** (desktop) shows the hosts' load only; its quota grid and the provider initials (V0.152 (5)) are gone.

(4) **Not changed:** the quota line under the provider picker of New thread on both fronts (V0.44), the provider cards in Settings (V0.77), the quota list in the phone's host-load sheet (V0.166 (2)), and how the host reads quota.

(5) **No cost.** The end of a turn says "Finished in 14 min"; a failed turn shows how long it ran on a tap. No dollar figure, on either front and for every provider. The host no longer writes `costUsd` into `run.ended`; an older event that has it is shown without it. The balance of a key provider (DeepSeek, OpenRouter) stays: it is that account's quota.

Tests: `web/test/quota.test.ts` (`busiestFigure`); `web/test/calm.test.ts` (the run label has no cost); `web/test/layout.e2e.mjs` V0.quota-in-thread, with `?mock=rail&hosts=2&open=t_mapperf`: desktop 1280 × 800, the chip reads as its windows, whole inside the header, with reset times on hover, the rail holds no quota and no turn end names a cost; phone 390 × 844 and 360 × 640, the title bar holds one whole figure, 44 px high, and a tap opens Details, which lists the windows, their resets and the age. V0.141, V0.152 and V0.167 no longer expect the rail's grid.
