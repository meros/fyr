# V0.depth-polish: surfaces get an order and controls an edge, and the warm colours lose their yellow cast

→ V0.warm-polish (1, 3, 7) and the colours in base.css. *Amends V0.warm-polish (1): the neutrals keep their warm hue with less yellow in it, and the lifts are a ring, a contact shadow and an ambient one.*

Why: after V0.warm-polish the client was calm but read as one plane. Cards, panes and buttons differed by a hair of colour and a faint shadow, so nothing said which thing lies over which or what can be pressed. The sand neutrals and the brown amber also gave the light theme a yellow, slightly murky cast. Three rounds of design, each followed by a visual review of the mock screens (both fronts, light and dark), led to this finish. Sizes, positions and words do not change.

1. **Cleaner warm tokens.** Light: the page is `#f9f6f1`, a raised surface `#fffefc`, a well `#f3eee7`; lines follow. The amber that carries "your move" is brighter and less brown (`--beam-ink` `#a05800`, `--beam-icon` `#c47100`, `--beam-wash` `#fff5e6`). Dark: the page is a step darker (`#161412`) and a raised surface a step lighter (`#211e1b`), so cards stand off the page. Text colours keep WCAG AA on every surface they sit on. The PWA's theme colour follows `--surface`.
2. **Lifts in three parts.** `--lift-1` to `--lift-3` are each a 1 px ring, a tight contact shadow and a soft ambient shadow, in a warm brown (`--shade`) in the light and in black, with a faint light on the top edge, in the dark. Where a card has a lift its ring is the frame, so its 1 px border is transparent.
3. **An order of planes.** The list casts a soft shadow on the page, and a thread's header on its log. The search field and the phone's section strip are shallow wells; the chosen section is a raised pill on the strip.
4. **Controls have an edge.** A secondary button has a face that falls off a little towards its foot (`--face`), a top highlight and a contact shadow, on the desktop and the phone (Skip, Settle merged). A primary button has a lit top and a deeper shadow; in the dark it stays one flat cream. A disabled primary is a well, not a slab. A key cap has a foot.
5. **State by colour first.** A row's tile takes the wash of its state (amber asks, blue is blocked, red failed). The selected row is a warm tint with a faint amber edge in the light, and an amber tint without an outline in the dark. A PR chip that is ready or merged has a green wash, a failing one a red wash, in the list and in a thread's header on both fronts.
6. **Cards.** A question's options are raised cards with a ring and a foot. The question's amber header has a lit top edge. The owner's bubble is a clean amber tint. Code wells are warm, a step under their card. The composer shows its focus with a ring.
7. **Dialogs.** The page behind a dialog is darker and frosted.
8. **Where it lives.** The tokens are in `web/src/base.css`, the lifts, `--shade` and `--face` in `web/src/layout.css`, and the finish is the last section of `web/src/warm.css`.
9. **The design system follows.** `design/generators/make_ds.py` carries the client's colours (it still held the first slate palette), Geist, the radii (5, 10, 12 and 16 px, pills for chips) and the shadows as tokens (`lift-1`, `lift-2`, `shadow-overlay`, `edge-control`, `edge-primary`, `well`); its components and brand book state the rule of planes and edges in place of "hairlines, not shadows". `design/system/` is generated from it. The README's images (`docs/images/`) are shot again. The dated direction mocks under `design/` (mobile, settings, changes-v2, rogue-agents, screens-v1) stay as the records they are.
