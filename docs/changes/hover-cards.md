# V0.hover-cards: the other long tooltips read as cards or plain words

→ V0.44, V0.77 (1), V0.quota-in-thread, V0.164, V0.176, V0.86, V0.137, V0.163, V0.load-card. *Amends the tooltip of each: the figures stay, their words and layout change.* (V0.load-card)

*Cause:* the browser's own `title` held whole reports: the quota's windows, reset times, age, stale note, error and next try as six lines of one size; the rail's undeclared and leftover buttons as one run-on sentence with every thread or process in it; and the heavy-job texts in measure words ("its share of the host (the larger of CPU and memory)", "above its idle level", "admit").

(1) **Three hover cards,** drawn like V0.load-card's (one `CardView` and one `HoverCard` portal in `web/src/card-ui.tsx`; the words pure in `card.ts` and each builder). **Quota** (the thread header's chip, the new-thread dialog's line, the provider's Usage row in Settings): the provider's name and a verdict in its colour, **Plenty left**, **Running low** (from 80 %), **Used up** (100 %) or, without data, the error's word (**Login expired**, **Rate limited**…), with one sentence when it is low or used up ("New runs on this account wait until 14:00."); then a row per window, "5 hours · 34 % used · resets 13:08", the balance, the **Problem** and its next try once (V0.77 (1)); "Read 2 min ago" (or "From the last run, …") last, with "may be out of date" when stale. **The rail's undeclared button:** "Heavy work Fyr didn't schedule", one sentence ("These 2 threads run heavy jobs without asking first. Fyr counts them against laptop's heavy-job budget."), each thread with its project, use and for how long, and what a click does. **The rail's leftovers:** "Still running after their thread", their count and memory in one sentence, each process with its count and memory, heaviest first, and "End or keep them in Settings → Automation." A rail card opens past the rail's edge; the quota's under its chip or line, above it when there is no room below.

(2) **Plain words,** still a short tooltip: the row's undeclared mark ("Heavy work Fyr didn't schedule: 2.8 GB and 2.1 cores more than at rest, for 3 min."), the row's CPU ("CPU this thread and what it started use right now"), waiting job ("Heavy jobs such as tests and builds wait until the host has room"), running job ("…and how much of the host it takes"), Waiting to run's header ("Heavy jobs wait here until the host has room. The first in line starts next."), the offline host ("The host is offline. This is the last copy Fyr saw; what you send waits until it is back.", desktop chip and phone line), the inbox's host filter ("desk is online, 12 threads in view. Click to hide its threads."), and the two Restart buttons (deploy banner, stuck agent).

(3) **Phone** (no hover): the same cards as text. A quota line's tap opens its card under it (the new-thread screen, the load sheet's quotas); the thread's Details → Quota is the card; the load sheet shows the leftovers card under each host's load card, the undeclared threads being in that card already.

Tests: `web/test/quota.test.ts`, `undeclared.test.ts`, `leftovers.test.ts` read the cards (`cardLines`); the layout test's V0.164 hovers the undeclared button, V0.176 the leftovers button and reads the phone sheet's card, V0.quota-in-thread hovers the chip and reads Details' card.
