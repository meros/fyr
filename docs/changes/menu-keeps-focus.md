# V0.menu-keeps-focus: an open menu keeps the keyboard's place when its entries change under it

→ V0.26, V0.31, V0.dialogs-review, V0.layout-v026-waits. The layout check "the ⋯ menu does not focus its first entry" still failed now and then after V0.layout-v026-waits made it wait for the focus. The failure message showed the focus on the page's body with the menu open. It was not a short wait: it was a bug in the menu.

1. **The cause.** A menu draws each entry by its id. A thread that changes while its menu is open can swap one entry for another: 1.5 s after a thread opens the app marks it seen (6.4), and "Mark seen" (`markSeen`) becomes "Mark unseen" (`markUnseen`). The button of the old entry leaves the page; when it had the focus, the focus falls to the body, and the menu's keys (arrows, Esc, an entry's letter) no longer reach the menu, so the inbox shortcuts act instead. On the thread the check opens, "Mark seen" is the first entry that is on, so it is the one that gets the focus. The check failed when a slow page had the menu open at the moment the thread was marked seen.
2. **The fix.** A menu remembers which entry has the focus. After a render that left the focus on nothing, it gives the focus to the entry now in that place (the last one when the list got shorter). A focus that went elsewhere on purpose is not taken back. One hook, `useKeepMenuFocus` in `web/src/menu-ui.tsx`, for the thread header's ⋯ menu and the row's right-click menu. The phone shows these actions in a sheet that is tapped, with no keyboard and no focus to keep, so it has no counterpart there.
3. **The checks.** A new layout section, V0.menu-keeps-focus, opens the ⋯ menu, focuses the seen entry, changes the thread under it and checks that the entry in its place has the focus, that ArrowDown moves on and that Esc closes the menu. The V0.26 check now reads the focused entry and the first entry in one read, since the first entry can change between two.
4. **How it was found.** The V0.26 section alone: 0 of 20 runs failed, and 0 of 20 beside 12 busy loops; the page is ready long before the 1.5 s, so the section alone does not meet the race. The whole layout suite beside 8 busy loops: this check failed in 1 of 8 runs. A log of every focus event, `focus()` call and DOM change in that run showed the menu's own `focus()` reach the entry, then a focusout to nothing, then that button removed and another inserted in one render.
5. **Before and after.** With the menu held open for 3 s before the check reads the focus, so the app's own mark-seen lands under it: 10 of 10 runs failed before the fix, 0 of 10 after. The new section fails in every run without the fix. The whole suite beside 8 busy loops after the fix: this check failed in 0 of 8 runs; the machine was less busy in most of those runs than in the runs before, so that count alone proves little.
6. **Not fixed here.** In those loaded runs the suite's 7 minute hang guard ended 4 of 16 runs while other work kept the machine busy, and "a reopened New thread shows no line about its draft" failed once (still open, as V0.layout-v026-waits says). Neither is this bug.
