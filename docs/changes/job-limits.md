# V0.job-limits: a job sizes itself: its cgroup gets a memory.high and a cpu.max from its class, and the CLI gets shares of the host for the tools that ignore cgroups

→ decision 0036 (C), slice 3; amends decision 0029 "S5 as built" (V0.151), V0.80's tree and V0.164 section 7. Builds on V0.load-tuning (the learned peak and demand) and decision 0032 slice 3 (the swap valve).

(1) **The CLI moves to a leaf.** A thread's cgroup (`fyr-agent-<thread>.scope` in the scope layout of V0.agents-unit, `fyr.service/agents/<thread>/` in the unit layout) now has the CLI in `cli/`, beside `job/` and `undeclared/`. In the unit layout the CLI starts in `cli/`. A scope starts with the CLI in its own cgroup (`systemd-run --scope`), so the sampler's next tick, or the first move of a job, moves the scope's processes into `cli/`; the scope is delegated, so fyrd may write its `cgroup.subtree_control`. A legacy `agents/<thread>` from before the switch gets the same split. The thread's cgroup enables `cpu` and `memory` for its children, so a job's cgroup has limit files of its own. The kernel then lets no process sit in the thread's cgroup itself (the no-internal-process rule), which is why the CLI cannot stay there. A CLI an older fyrd started in the thread's cgroup moves to `cli/` when fyrd takes the thread again after a restart. If the kernel refuses (a process could not move), fyrd logs `guard: job limit off thread=…` and the thread runs as before, without job limits. The job is still found by its command line among the thread's processes (now those of `cli/`), the guard, the turns of decision 0032 slice 2 and the leftovers read `cli/` too, and a thread's `memory.high` (decision 0032 slice 3), CPU time and stall are read from the thread's cgroup as before, since they count its children.

(2) **The limits.** When fyrd moves an admitted job into `job/`, or the guard moves undeclared work into `undeclared/`, it writes on that cgroup:
- `memory.high` = the class's learned peak × 1.25. A class not learned yet gets the agents' free memory, at most half the agents' budget. There is no `memory.max`, so the limit slows a job and never kills it. Node (libuv) sizes its heap from the smaller of `memory.max` and `memory.high` of its own cgroup.
- `cpu.max` = the class's learned demand × 1.25, rounded up, at least 1 core, none when that is all the agents' cores or the class is not learned. Go 1.25's GOMAXPROCS (so `go test -p`) and Jest follow it. The margin is there because a cap at the demand itself could only ratchet the learned demand down: a job held to its cap can never show more.
- The kernel does not move a process's memory with it: what a job allocated before fyrd moved it stays charged to `cli/`, and its limit counts what it allocates after the move. A job is moved on the sampler's first tick after it starts, so that is most of it. A `memory.high` below what the cgroup already holds is written off the guard's tick, because the write reclaims before it returns.
- The parts of undeclared work (`undeclared/u<pid>/`) get no limit of their own: the runner stays in `undeclared/` while its parts take turns, so `undeclared/` cannot enable controllers for them. The limit on `undeclared/` bounds them all.

(3) **The swap valve.** Every job limit lifts (`max`) while the agents' swap is at 90 % of its cap, and comes back below it, as the thread shares of decision 0032 slice 3 do: `memory.high` with no swap room livelocks.

(4) **Learning.** A job whose `memory.events` `high` count rises hit its limit: its class's learned peak is raised × 1.2 once per job (as VPA does after an overrun), and the job's own record raises it further when it ends.

(5) **The tools that ignore cgroups.** fyrd starts each agent CLI with shares of what the host gives its agents (decision 0029 section 0: no absolute numbers): `GOMEMLIMIT` = half the agents' memory budget, and `PYTEST_XDIST_AUTO_NUM_WORKERS` (`pytest -n auto`) = half the agents' cores, at least 1. A profile's own variables win. Playwright's test runner and turbo read no variable for their workers, so the gate hook adds a notice to a `playwright test` without `--workers`/`-j` and to a `turbo` run without `--concurrency`, naming the flag and the same worker count. The command is not rewritten (decision 0029, S5 as built).

(6) **The log.** `guard: job limit thread=… cgroup=job|undeclared class=… high=… cpu=… why=learned|unknown|swap-full|no-budget learned_peak=… learned_demand=… agents_mem=…/… was=…` on each change, and `guard: job limit hit thread=… class=… high=… mem=… high_events=+… learned_peak=…` once per job.
