# V0.phone-row-moves: on the phone the open row does not keep its place

→ V0.open-row-only. *Amends V0.open-row-only (2) and (4): the open row keeps its place on the desktop only.* (owner, 19 Nov: "On mobile, when going back to the list, don't hold the position of the "selected" card. You can't see list and card at the same time".)

(1) **The phone.** The list and the thread are never on screen together, so holding the open row's place shows nothing the owner was looking at. Back on the list, every row, the last open one too, is where the live list has it.

(2) **The desktop** is unchanged: the current thread keeps the section and place it had when it was opened, until another thread is current. The difference is the screen, not a choice: the desktop shows the list beside the thread.
