# V0.composer-focus-reach: one composer for both fronts, one focus trap for every dialog, and every desktop click target a button

A review found the composer written twice (desktop and phone repeated the draft, attachments, the send guard, the `/` list, the field's growth and the send, and differed in the height cap, Enter and the Stop label with no reason given), dialogs that said `aria-modal` but let Tab walk into the inbox behind them, and desktop controls that only a mouse could press.

1. **One `useComposer`** (`web/src/composer.tsx`) holds the composer's state and commands; `Composer` (desktop) and `PhoneComposer` are two thin views over it, side by side. What a key does and the placeholder are pure (`web/src/compose.ts`, `test/composer.test.ts`). A send the host took clears the field and the chips and gives the haptic tap on both (a no-op without a touch screen). The differences left say why next to the code:
   - Enter sends on the desktop; on the phone it is a new line, because the on-screen return key is the only way to start one, and Send is the button.
   - The desktop composer is also the question's answer field; the phone's question sheet has its own field and the composer is hidden under it.
   - The run's stop is named **Stop** on both (the desktop said Interrupt). The desktop shows it beside Send with Esc; the phone puts it in Send's one 44 px slot while the field is empty.
   - The field grows to its CSS `max-height`, the one place the cap is set: 10 lines on the desktop, 5 on the phone, where the keyboard takes half the screen. The placeholder is "Steer the run…" on both.
2. **One focus trap** (`web/src/focus-trap.ts`, `useFocusTrap`) in `DialogScrim` and `BottomSheet`, so in every dialog, sheet and the confirmation: on open the focus moves into the panel (to its first control on the desktop; to the panel itself as a sheet, since a focused field would raise the phone's keyboard before a touch), Tab and ⇧Tab wrap inside it, and on close the focus goes back to what had it. Nested panels: only the newest traps. The confirmation's own Tab handling is gone.
3. **Buttons, not clickable text.** On the desktop the thread's tabs are buttons with `role=tab` and `aria-selected` (the phone's tabs gain the same roles); the change chip, "Question 1 of 3" (when there is more than one), a subagent's prompt, the Agent row and the grip row of Settings → Projects are buttons; the question's options are a radio group (checkboxes for multi-select) with `aria-checked`, as on the phone. The look does not change.

Tests: `test/composer.test.ts`, `test/focus-trap.test.ts`, and in `test/layout.e2e.mjs` the sections "dialog focus" (Tab stays in the confirmation, Esc gives the focus back to the chip that opened it) and "keyboard reach" (tabs, options, Enter on a tab).
