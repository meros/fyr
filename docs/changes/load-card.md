# V0.load-card: a host's load reads as a card, not a wall of measures

→ V0.80 (6), V0.99 (3), V0.142 (7), V0.146 (5), V0.163, V0.164, V0.166, V0.167. *Amends the tooltip of each: the figures stay, their words and layout change.* 

*Cause:* the rail's host tooltip was the browser's own `title`: twelve lines of one size and colour, each a measure name (load average, CPU pressure, PSI stall, provider) with its unit and window in brackets, and the heavy jobs as one long sentence that wrapped mid-figure. Nothing said whether the host was fine.

(1) **Verdict first.** The card opens with the host's name and its level in its colour, **Calm**, **Busy** or **Overloaded** (the ring's level, V0.99 and decision 0029), and one sentence of what it means for new work: "New jobs run 3.1× slower than on an idle machine." (with "Memory is short." or "Memory is getting tight." after it at a critical or elevated stall); a host without a slowdown says its load as a share of the cores.

(2) **Two groups of rows,** each row a label, a value and a quieter note; a hot value in the signal colour. **Machine**: Load ("530 % of 8 cores", note "42 now · 39 over 5 min · 31 over 15 min"), Speed ("3.1× slower than idle", for a new job), CPU waits (the CPU pressure as "81 % of the time", note the agents' share), Memory ("28 % free of 9.7 GB", note "no stalls", "stalling 6.0 %" or "stalling hard 12.0 %"), Agents use ("6.0 of 6 cores · 8.9 of 11.6 GB"). **Heavy jobs**: CPU and Memory ("102 % of the host", note "budget 50 % · 32 % undeclared"; hot past the budget), Budget (why it moved), Running (one row per job, its CPU and memory), Waiting (how many and why the next waits), Undeclared (one row per thread, with its project, its use and for how long). A host that counts jobs says "2 of 2 jobs · counted, not measured". "Measured by cgroups" is the card's last line, the meters' legend ("ld", "job") its foot.

(3) **Desktop:** the card opens beside the rail on hover and on keyboard focus of a host's meters (the meters are now focusable), on the page's body so the rail's containment does not clip it, kept inside the window. The job meter's own tooltip is gone: the card holds what it said. **Phone:** the load sheet shows the same card under each host's meters (no hover on a phone), and the legend once after the hosts. One component, `LoadCardView` in `web/src/load-ui.tsx`; the words are pure in `loadCard` (`web/src/load.ts`).

Tests: `web/test/load.test.ts`, `load-signals.test.ts`, `admit.test.ts`, `percent.test.ts`, `undeclared.test.ts` read the card's rows; the layout test's V0.80 and V0.164 sections hover a host and read the card, V0.166 reads it in the phone's sheet.
