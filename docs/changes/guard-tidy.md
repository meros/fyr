# V0.guard-tidy: the memory guard does not reclaim near oomd's limit, releases an emptied job, freezes only work that holds the memory, never roots a restarted CLI, lifts undeclared/'s limit during turns, holds past 10 min only on the agents' stall and at most 30 min, logs a job's freeze like undeclared work's, rotates nothing after an episode ended, and sends one notice per event

→ V0.guard-swap, V0.oomd-guard (4), V0.job-limits, V0.joblimit-slice, V0.freeze-old-work, V0.episode-frozen, V0.guard-agents-stall, V0.rerun-classes, decision 0032 slice 1 and 2 and "What the log gives for tuning", decision 0036 (E2) and "Not chosen".

(1) **No reclaim near oomd's limit.** After a freeze, and on every turn, `ReclaimFrozen` (V0.guard-swap) writes the frozen work's `memory.reclaim`. Decision 0036 "Not chosen" says why that is wrong while oomd watches an ancestor: a reclaim raises the cgroup's pgscan, so oomd picks the work fyrd just reclaimed, and with `memory.oom.group=1` on the thread's scope it kills the paused thread whole. While oomd's limit is known and the unit's full stall is within 10 points of it (the margin of V0.guard-agents-stall), the reclaim is skipped, with one line a minute: `guard: no reclaim of t_…'s frozen work near oomd's limit: unit_full=55% limit=60%; a reclaim would make it oomd's first pick`. Away from the limit it stays, for V0.guard-swap's reasons.

(2) **An emptied job is released.** A frozen job whose `job/` holds no process any more (`cgroup.events` says `populated 0`, or the cgroup is gone; on a host without cgroups, every stopped process is gone) is thawed at once with `reason="its processes are gone"`, as undeclared work already was. Before, it held one of the guard's freezes (the running threads less one) until the 10 min limit.

(3) **Only work that holds the memory is frozen.** On 19 Nov at 16:02 the guard made 3 parts of a thread's work take turns while each read `mem=0.0 GB`: the thread's 3.3 GB was charged to its `cli/`, since the kernel does not move memory with a process. Freezing them freed nothing. Now, when the resident memory of the episode's roots and their descendants is under 25 % of the thread's flagged memory, the guard neither freezes the work nor starts turns, and says so once a minute: `guard: not freezing thread=t_…: the work holds little of the thread's memory (its processes 0.0 GB of 3.3 GB)`.

(4) **A restarted CLI is never a root.** A CLI restarted during an episode (a handover) started after the episode's first over reading, so it read as new work and was a root: frozen, it took its MCP servers with it. The CLI is now never a root. Of its children, only the Bash shell it starts for a command (`shell-snapshots/snapshot-…`) is a root; the rest (its MCP servers) stay residents. V0.freeze-old-work already kept the CLI out of the elders.

(5) **undeclared/'s limit lifts during turns.** `undeclared/` holds the frozen parts as well as the one whose turn it is, so its `memory.high` (V0.job-limits) counted the frozen parts' memory against the part that runs. And only `FreezeUndeclared` sized it; a rotation never did. While the parts take turns the limit is `max` (log: `guard: job limit … high=max … why=turns`); when the turns end (the thaw) the kept or learned limit is written back.

(6) **Past 10 min, the agents' stall and a ceiling.** V0.oomd-guard (4) kept frozen work past the 10 min limit while the host's or the agents' memory stalled critically. The host's stall can come from the desktop, which the frozen work does not feed, so only the agents' own critical stall holds it now. And the hold ends at 30 min: the work thaws with `reason="30 min ceiling, the agents' memory still stalls critically"`, and the thread is told it runs again. Work of a rerunnable class is not thawed at the ceiling: V0.rerun-classes kills it 2 min into its freeze, and a class capped at 3 kills an hour is frozen and thawed like any other.

(7) **The log and the history.** `guard: froze job` now has the fields of `guard: froze undeclared work` that decision 0032's log section names: `freeze=1/1` (a hold is frozen once), `episode_age=` (the hold's age), `cmd=` (its class), `cores=` (its need) and `running=`. Work kept past the limit is a `kept` event in the guard's history, so Settings → Automation → Memory guard (desktop and phone, one component) shows "Kept undeclared work of … frozen past 10 minutes: the agents' memory stalls critically", and a thaw at the ceiling reads "Resumed …: frozen 30 minutes, the most the guard holds work".

(8) **No rotation after the episode ended.** The detector ends an episode on the sampler's goroutine and `EndUndeclared` thaws and removes `undeclared/u*/`; the engine's tick rotates the turns. A rotation that read the flags before the end made and froze `u*/` again after it. A rotation now checks, under a lock that `EndUndeclared` also takes, that the detector still flags the thread, and does nothing if not.

(9) **One notice per event.** When memory turned short, the job that grew most heard "Memory is short … nothing is stopped", and on the same tick the guard froze it and it heard "Fyr paused your heavy job". The memory notice now comes after the freeze and is not sent to a thread the guard holds frozen.
