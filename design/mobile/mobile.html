Fyr on the phone

Mocks for the translation rules in README.md, at 390 × 844 with iOS safe areas (47 px top, 34 px bottom). Rail tokens, Geist, and the nine state icons. Magenta numbered dots are tap counts, not UI. Open mobile.html#q1-light (any frame id) to see one frame alone.

1 · Inbox

The rail becomes the count strip: the same five sections, icons and counts, in the same order, under the header. The list stays one list (U1). Rows grow from 52 to 68 px and from 30 to 48 px.

2 · Section switch (g1 to g5)

A strip segment scrolls the one list to its section and expands it if collapsed. It never filters, so counts and order stay explainable (U12).

3 · Row actions (e, s, #, u, x, Shift+E, Space)

One swipe per direction, long press for peek and every action, selection mode for bulk. Every deferred action ends in a 5 s Undo toast (U7).

4 · Thread

Back, title and ⋯ on top; chips and tabs below; the composer at the bottom. The keyboard collapses the header to one row.

5 · Question card (A2)

The docked card fills the thread below the header. One question per step, full-width tiles, one sticky commit button.

6 · Approval and Fyr action cards

The same card: pick a tile, then the commit button that names the pick.

7 · New thread from a screenshot (A3)

Paste and Photos sit on top. Project and profile default to the last used on this device.

8 · Snooze sheet (s)

A bottom sheet with the five presets and exact times.

9 · Notifications

Push only for asking and failed; done and blocked only as a batch after 10 min away (U6).

10 · Installed-app states

Install, bell, connection and update states in the inbox header.
